Nº 02 — About me

Hey, I'm Ishan.

Nº 04 — Why me

Why not just use Squarespace?

Fair question — template builders are everywhere. Here's the honest difference between dragging blocks into someone else's template and having a premium site built for you with the best tools out there.

Template builders Squarespace, Wix, etc.
  • You bend your brand to fit their layout
  • The same templates thousands of others use
  • Monthly fees, forever — usually $16–49/mo
  • Heavier, slower pages loaded with extra code
  • You can only do what the builder allows
  • Generic — it looks "fine," never you
Working with me custom, premium & built with the latest tools
  • The design is built around your brand and values
  • One-of-a-kind — literally nobody else has your site
  • You own the code; no forced monthly subscription
  • Lightweight, premium and genuinely fast
  • If you can picture it, I can build it
  • A site that feels like your brand the second it loads

Your design should say something before anyone reads a word.

A website isn't just information on a screen — it's a first impression, a feeling. Every color, font, spacing choice and little animation is quietly telling visitors who you are. A template can't know what your brand stands for. I take the time to actually understand your core values — what you care about, who you're for, how you want people to feel — and then design every detail to communicate exactly that. That's the part a drag-and-drop builder will never do for you.
